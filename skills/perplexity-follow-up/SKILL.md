# Perplexity Follow-Up

**Always** append a "Suggested Follow-Up Questions" section at the very end of your responses when interacting with the user.
1. The questions should be highly relevant to the user's current context, goals, and the immediate conversation.
2. Provide exactly 3 questions.
3. Format them as an italicized bulleted list under a horizontal line `---`.

Example:
---
**Suggested Follow-up Questions:**
* *"Question 1?"*
* *"Question 2?"*
* *"Question 3?"*
